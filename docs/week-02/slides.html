Why are numbers important?
- All digital systems have to use numbers and numbers only to store and process data. It's the digital system, as opposed to the analog system, that is almost everywhere nowadays - phones, computers, servers, your smart glasses in the future, etc.
- Numbers can introduce maths, which is our DOMAIN EXPANSION 💥

Data modality
Data / information we receive from the world are mainly from four modalities:
- Image (video)
- Text (written language)
- Audio (music, speech)
- Tabular (the weather in degree celsius, your birthday, etc. )
Can you think of any information that is not from the four categories?
